Cheesy Ramen Noodles

Description

A common quick fix instant noodle that is slightly spicy, with soup and a stable korean household or just any household actually. Served with American cheese.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 (3 ounce) package any flavor ramen soup noodles
  • 1 slice American cheese

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring water to a boil in a saucepan. Add ramen onodles and cook until tender, about 2 minutes.
  3. Pour out water, then stir in seasoning packet and cheese until well blended.
